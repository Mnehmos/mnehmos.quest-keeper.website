01 / CREATE A WORLD

Name the campaign, describe its setting and premise, and choose the tone. The DM authors the places, people, and threats as the story unfolds.

02 / MAKE A CHARACTER

Choose a supported species, class, background, alignment, abilities, skills, languages, and tools. The engine records the resulting sheet.

03 / SAY WHAT YOU DO

Describe actions in ordinary language. When a choice has mechanical consequences, the DM uses the RPG MCP tools and then narrates the committed result.

Free to begin. The free tier lets you enter the table. The optional Player Pass provides more room for a persistent campaign. Usage is shown in the player UI as a plan-relative percentage.
